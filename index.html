Profile

I direct the undergraduate Economics program and coordinate the Applied Economics and Data Science concentration at Tecnológico de Monterrey. My work includes teaching, academic administration, research, and economic consulting.

I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Public Policy. I hold an M.A. in Economics from El Colegio de México and a B.A. in Economics from Tecnológico de Monterrey.
